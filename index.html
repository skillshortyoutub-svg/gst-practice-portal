GST Law (summary for students)

CGST Act 2017, SGST Acts, IGST Act 2017. Intra-state supply: CGST + SGST (half each). Inter-state supply: IGST. Rate slabs after the rate rationalisation effective 22 Sep 2025: 5%, 18%, 40%, plus 0%, 0.25% and 3%; the old 12% and 28% slabs remain selectable here for older practice periods.
